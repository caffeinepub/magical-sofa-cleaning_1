# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Update the Magical Service landing page with a new sofa price calculator range, vibrating/glowing floating contact icons, a prominent hero heading, and a refreshed light color palette across all sections.

**Planned changes:**
- Update the SofaPriceCalculator to use a scrollable range input from 5 to 30 feet (1-foot increments) priced at 90 Rs per foot, with live price updates
- Add a prominent "Best Sofa Cleaning Ahmedabad" heading at the very top of the page/Hero section in white text
- Add continuous vibrate (shake/wiggle) and glowing/shining animations to both the Call and WhatsApp floating contact buttons
- Refresh all major section backgrounds with varied attractive light colors (soft mint, warm ivory, light peach, pale lavender, sky tones, etc.) so each section is visually distinct; keep cards and text areas white/near-white

**User-visible outcome:** Visitors see a bold heading at the top, can use a smooth foot-range price calculator, notice eye-catching animated contact buttons, and experience a modern, airy multi-color section layout throughout the page.
